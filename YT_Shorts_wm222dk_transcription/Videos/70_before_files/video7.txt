→ Unsupported — this is a personality/values-based generalization not found in DSM-5-TR or ICD-11 criteria for either ADHD or autism spectrum disorder. → Unsupported — this is a value-laden characterization, not a diagnostic or clinically verifiable claim, and it generalizes a personality disposition to all people with ADHD or autism
