→ Unsupported — this specific mechanistic model of ADHD (a sensory-encoding failure rather than importance-based prioritization) is not described in DSM-5-TR or ICD-11; ADHD is classified as a neurodevelopmental disorder involving inattention and hyperactivity-impulsivity clusters, not framed in the attached references as a distinct "sensory attentional" processing deficit

Claim 2: Neurotypical people also forget things and don't pay attention 100% of the time, but they don't forget major events like exams, projects, or weddings
→ Unsupported — this specific comparative claim about neurotypical memory reliability for major life events is not addressed in DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or NICE

→ Partially supported — the frequency component aligns with ICD-11's "boundary with normality" framing (symptoms like forgetfulness occur in everyone but must be persistent/frequent to indicate ADHD) and its inattention criteria ("being forgetful in daily activities"); however, the specific claim about consequence severity being systematically worse is not something ICD-11 or NICE quantifies or states directly

→ Unsupported — this specific psychological/emotional mechanism is not described in DSM-5-TR or ICD-11; while emotional dysregulation and low frustration tolerance are noted as associated features, a chronic "dread state" tied to self-distrust when relaxed is not part of the diagnostic or clinical picture in the attached references
