→ Unsupported — media/short-form content consumption habits are not a diagnostic criterion in DSM-5-TR or ICD-11; while ADHD is associated with seeking high-stimulation, high-reward activities (ICD-11 notes inattention "may not be evident when engaged in activities that provide intense stimulation and frequent rewards"), singling out a specific app/platform as a diagnostic sign is an overgeneralization not grounded in the clinical criteria
