Claims identified & fact-check:

Claim 1 (framing claim): There are 14 "official" ADHD behaviours, and you need all 14 for a diagnosis
→ Unsupported — ICD-11 does not specify a fixed count of "14 required behaviours" for diagnosis, nor does it (or DSM-5-TR, as referenced in NICE's context section) require meeting all listed symptoms; diagnosis requires "several symptoms" from inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity clusters, not a complete checklist of every possible item — this framing materially misrepresents how the criteria work

Claim 2: Trouble sitting still
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "difficulty sitting still without fidgeting"

Claim 3: Quickly distracted by different stimulating things
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "being easily distracted by extraneous stimuli or thoughts not related to the task at hand"

Claim 4: Difficulty concentrating on things that don't interest you
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "difficulty sustaining attention on tasks that do not provide a high level of stimulation or reward"

Claim 5: Quickly switching from one activity to another
→ Unsupported — not a listed ICD-11 inattention or hyperactivity-impulsivity criterion; task-switching itself is not among the described clusters

Claim 6: Can't keep internally still
→ Supported — matches ICD-11's note on "feelings of physical restlessness" in adolescents/adults

Claim 7: Can't wait your turn in group settings
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "having difficulty waiting their turn in conversation, games or activities"

Claim 8: Often knowing/blurting out the answer to a question
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "blurting out answers in school"

Claim 9: Trouble fulfilling/completing tasks
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "not completing tasks"

Claim 10: Difficulty trying to play calmly
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "having difficulty engaging in activities quietly"

Claim 11: Often not thinking through before doing things
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "acting in response to immediate stimuli without deliberation... impulsive decisions"

Claim 12: Often losing things
→ Supported — matches ICD-11 directly: "losing things"

Claim 13: Frequently disrupting/interrupting others
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "interrupting or intruding on others' conversations or games"

Claim 14: Talking a lot
→ Supported — matches ICD-11: "talking too much"

Claim 15: Often not seeming to be listening
→ Supported — matches ICD-11 directly: "often seeming not to listen when spoken to directly"
