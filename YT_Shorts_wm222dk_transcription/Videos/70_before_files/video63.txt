Claim 1: Some amount of sensory input is necessary for some brains to maintain focus
→ Supported (externally verified) — matches the well-documented "optimal stimulation theory"/"moderate brain arousal model" research base, confirmed across multiple peer-reviewed studies showing ADHD brains often operate in an under-aroused state that benefits from added sensory input

Claim 2: Studies show that people with ADHD find it easier to maintain focus when there's white noise or music in the background
→ Supported (externally verified) — confirmed by multiple peer-reviewed studies (e.g., a 2007 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry study, replicated in 2010; a 2022 pilot study on verbal working memory; a 2024 Communications Biology/Nature study on modulated music improving sustained attention), though it's worth noting effects aren't universal — one cited 2024 study found about a third of ADHD participants performed worse with white noise, so individual variation exists
